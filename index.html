Automatic Sanitization

The system automatically dispenses sanitizer while operating the door handle, improving hygiene without requiring additional effort from users.

Smart Mechanical Design

A spring-based mechanical linkage activates sanitizer flow during normal door operation without using electricity or complex electronics.

Public Hygiene Solution

Designed for hospitals, schools, offices, railway stations, malls, and other public environments with high human contact.

About The Product

The Self-Sanitizing Door Handle is an innovative hygiene-focused mechanical system that automatically dispenses sanitizer while operating the door handle. The system integrates sanitization directly into normal human behavior without requiring additional effort from users.

No Electricity

Mechanical operation without external power.

User Friendly

Automatic sanitization during normal usage.

Why This Product Is Required

Public Hygiene Problem

Door handles are among the most frequently touched surfaces and major sources of contamination.

People Forget Sanitizer

Manual sanitization systems are often ignored by users.

Need For Automation

Hygiene systems should become automatic and integrated into everyday behavior.

Public Safety

Hospitals, offices, schools, malls and stations require improved hygiene systems.

How The Product Works

Step 1

User grips the handle.

Step 2

Internal linkage activates automatically.

Step 3

User opens the door safely with sanitized hands.

Design Thinking Journey

Observation

Observed hygiene issues in public spaces and contaminated door handles.

User Research

Conducted surveys and studied public hygiene behavior.

Ideation

Brainstormed multiple engineering concepts and selected the most practical solution.

Prototype Development

Developed and tested the functional mechanical prototype.

Refinement

Improved design using mentor feedback and engineering analysis.

Materials & Mechanism

Medical Grade Silicone

Ensures smooth sanitizer flow and prevents clogging.
